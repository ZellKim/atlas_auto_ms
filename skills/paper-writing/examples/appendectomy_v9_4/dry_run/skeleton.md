## Section 2: Methods (~700 w in full, 1-paragraph skeleton)

- Data source: federated OMOP CDM network of 15 Korean tertiary hospitals [refpack:Hripcsak2015, Overhage2012, Voss2015]; period 2002-01-01 to 2024-12-31; OMOP CDM v5.3; analysis code executed locally per site; pre-match analytic outputs returned for central IPD pooling; raw records never leave institutional boundary; IRB KHNMC 2025-10-009 with consent waiver; STROBE guidance.
- Population: adults ≥18 with first occurrence of appendectomy (target, concept 4198190) / cholecystectomy (4242997, first active comparator) / hernia repair (4196678, 4244809, second active comparator) between 2002-01-01 and 2024-12-31; ≥365 d prior observation, ≥1 d post; cohort-specific neoplasm exclusion + 8-concept concurrent major abdominal surgery exclusion.
- Outcomes: 30 prespecified biomarker abnormality endpoints (first post-index measurement crossing prespecified threshold) across cellular-immune / inflammatory / hepatic / metabolic / hematologic / endocrine / renal; 5 bidirectional analytes; 3 pooled endpoints for cross-recording variants. 11 prespecified clinical disease outcomes. **54 prespecified negative controls** from OHDSI LEGEND framework [refpack:Suchard2019] drawing on Voss / Ryan reference sets [refpack:Voss2017, Ryan2013].
- Analysis: Cyclops [refpack:Suchard2013] regularized logistic regression (Laplace prior, 10-fold CV) for per-comparator propensity scores; 1:1 nearest-neighbor matching on logit PS [refpack:Rosenbaum1983, Austin2011], caliper 0.2 SD; **365-to-30-day prior-abnormality-exclusion filter** symmetric across arms (preserves immediate 30-day preoperative window). **α primary window: 31–3,650 d postacute**; β secondary window: 1–3,650 d. Site-stratified Cox proportional-hazards regression [refpack:Austin2014] with standard partial-likelihood variance. Empirical calibration [refpack:Schuemie2018a, Schuemie2018b] per-comparator null fitted by method-of-moments; calibrated HR = exp(logHR − null_mean), calibrated SE = √(SE² + null_sd²). Sensitivity: LOSO (15 × 4), σ-grid (0.10/0.20/0.30), lookback (90/365/unlimited outer + 30-d inner skip), β secondary window, per-endpoint surveillance bound. Unified-PS, unified-matching, site-stratified-Cox IPD pooling extending prior Korean designs [refpack:Cha2024a, Lee2024, Cha2024b, Jung2026, Lim2026]. R 4.5.2 + OHDSI CohortMethod / Cyclops / FeatureExtraction / EmpiricalCalibration.

## Section 3: Results (~700 w in full, 1-paragraph skeleton)

- Cohort: pre-match 88,592 (chole pool) / 50,702 (hernia pool); after 365-to-30-d filter 77.5% / 80.8% retained; after 1:1 matching biomarker analytic cohorts **13,223 vs 13,221 (chole)** and **7,148 vs 7,149 (hernia)**, clinical cohorts 16,105 vs 16,102 and 9,184 vs 9,184 (Table 1; per-covariate SMD ≤0.10). Median follow-up 734/834 d (chole pair) / 1,048/1,034 d (hernia pair). Administrative censoring 12.4–16.3%. Empirical nulls: biomarker chole 0.076 (SD 0.100), hernia 0.181 (SD ≈0 boundary); clinical 0.064 / 0.154.
- Biomarker findings: **9/30 significant after calibration vs chole (7 protective, 2 elevated — ALT/AST chole-confounder)**; **18/30 vs hernia (17 protective)**. **Three-endpoint cellular-immune core** (lymphocyte% low / neutrophilia / WBC elevated): cHR 0.59–0.66 (chole), 0.23–0.33 (hernia), 15/15 LOSO retention in both. **Rate differences: 3–5 fewer events/1,000 PY vs chole, 11–20 fewer vs hernia**. CRP elevated as **supportive inflammatory**: cHR 0.46 (hernia, *P* <.001), 0.81 (chole, *P* =.055 boundary). Detail Figure 1, Table 2.
- Sensitivity: LOSO 15/15 retention for core + CRP in both comparators (eTable 4); σ-grid all three core retained sig vs hernia at σ ∈ {0.10, 0.20, 0.30}, vs chole at σ=0.20, retained protective direction without sig at σ=0.30 vs chole (eTable 6); lookback 18/9/12 of 30 sig vs chole, 12/18/15 vs hernia (eTable 5); β secondary 23/30 vs chole (dominated by acute hepatobiliary), 16/30 vs hernia (eFigure 3); App/Hernia measurement ratio ≥1.08 for core endpoints in 91–365 d and 1–3 yr windows (eTable 7).
- Clinical findings: 3/11 significant after calibration — atopic dermatitis cHR 0.62 (0.39–0.99) vs chole, type-2 diabetes cHR 1.35 (1.05–1.73) vs hernia, biliary calculus cHR 2.87 (2.03–4.05) vs chole (**surgical-indication contrast**, not biology). T2D **directionally discordant** with diabetic-range glucose (cHR 0.65 vs hernia) and HbA1c-elevated (cHR 0.72 vs chole) — exploratory. Other tested outcomes (Crohn, *C. difficile*, Parkinson) not individually significant.

## Section 4: Discussion (~900 w in full, 1-paragraph skeleton)

- §1 Principal: postacute α window — 9/30 (chole) and 18/30 (hernia) significant after calibration. Three-endpoint cellular-immune core (lymphocyte% low / neutrophilia / WBC elevated) significant + protective in both comparators (cHR 0.59–0.66 / 0.23–0.33; 15/15 LOSO; 11–20 fewer events/1,000 PY vs hernia, 3–5 vs chole). CRP elevated supportive inflammatory (strong vs hernia, boundary vs chole). Atopic dermatitis lower vs chole, T2D higher vs hernia (discordant with glucose laboratory — exploratory).
- §2 Comparison: Cellular-immune core consistent with appendix GALT immunology [refpack:Kooij2016, Spencer2016, Bollinger2007] but does not establish causation. Lower UC association [refpack:Andersson2001] and higher Crohn [refpack:Andersson2003, Kaplan2008] not directly observed (limited power). T2D direction concordant with Wei 2015 Taiwan cohort [refpack:Wei2015]. **Discordance ★**: 2025 systematic review found no overall Parkinson association [refpack:Chin2025] — our Parkinson cHR 0.93 / 1.23 not individually significant, broadly consistent with null but reinforces methodological heterogeneity. Active-comparator design extending Marras 2016 Ontario [refpack:Marras2016] from PD-specific to laboratory biomarker layer.
- §3 Mechanism: appendix as GALT lymphoid reservoir and microbiome modulator [refpack:Bollinger2007, Kooij2016, Cani2022, Gurung2020]. Mechanistic interpretation **limited by observational design** + laboratory testing being part of outcome ascertainment. T2D laboratory–disease discordance suggests diagnosis coding / medication initiation / testing patterns drive clinical signal beyond glycemic biology. Surveillance bound (eTable 7) — App/Hernia measurement ratio ≥1.08 in postacute windows — weighs against differential surveillance as explanation for protective hernia HRs. Vs chole, appendectomy patients accrued fewer long-term measurements: combined biology + selection + measurement-process. **Hernia comparator and cross-comparator core carry primary interpretive weight**.
- §4 Strengths and limitations (본문 흐름):
  - Strengths: postacute primary frame isolating long-term from acute recovery; dual active-comparator extending Marras 2016 to biomarker layer; empirical calibration against 54 LEGEND NCs [refpack:Suchard2019] with per-comparator null fitting — among first multi-site distributed-OMOP applications of empirical calibration to threshold-defined biomarker endpoints in this exposure; comprehensive sensitivity suite (β / lookback / σ-grid / LOSO / surveillance bound); per-endpoint absolute event-rate reporting; unified-PS / matching / Cox IPD pooling extending Korean designs [refpack:Cha2024a, Lee2024, Cha2024b, Jung2026, Lim2026].
  - Limitations: observational, causation precluded; chole-comparator estimates reflect biology + surgical-indication confounding (chole-attributable ALT/AST elevation) + long-term measurement-intensity differential — hernia + core less affected; Korean tertiary hospitals limit generalizability; OMOP CDM does not distinguish perforated from non-perforated appendicitis; standard partial-likelihood variance rather than sandwich variance clustered by matched pair (cross-comparator + large effects + 15/15 LOSO mitigates); calibration against clinical NCs addresses broad systematic error, not laboratory-specific measurement-process bias; only 3/11 clinical endpoints individually significant (one surgical-indication artifact), reflecting limited power.
- §5 Implications + closing: postacute monitoring may be clinically warranted in the 91–365 d window. Prospective immune cohorts with direct inflammatory markers + microbiota sampling needed for mechanism. For observational researchers, dual-comparator + empirical calibration framework demonstrated here offers tractable approach to postsurgical biomarker associations beyond acute window. **Findings are hypothesis-generating**; replication with harmonized outcome definitions and explicit measurement-process adjustment is needed.
