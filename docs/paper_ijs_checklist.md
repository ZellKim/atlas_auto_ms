### Highlights (초안)

1. Appendectomy causes sustained immune shift: reduced WBC, neutrophils, lymphocytes
2. Time-window analysis separates surgical stress from appendix-specific effects
3. First multi-center biomarker evidence from 14 institutions using OMOP CDM
4. Immune changes persist beyond 91 days, with transient allergic-type elevation
5. Empirical calibration with 48 negative controls confirms robustness
